Groundwater Recharge Zones, Bhopal live

Nov 2026

Python · FastAPI · rasterio · GeoPandas · AHP · Leaflet

Semester team project at MANIT Bhopal (Civil Engineering). The map shows where rain is most likely to soak in and recharge groundwater across Bhopal, Raisen and Narmadapuram districts. Seven thematic layers — rainfall, geology, slope, soil, land use, drainage density and lineament density — are ranked and combined with weights from the Analytic Hierarchy Process. You can pick a tehsil or watershed, switch between pre-monsoon, monsoon and post-monsoon views, change the weights, and export the result as a GeoTIFF or PDF.

Loading the map… the server sleeps when nobody uses it, so the first visit can take up to a minute.

Results

Study area17,608 km², 15 tehsils, 483 micro-watersheds
Resolution30 m grid
Validation60 CGWB wells, Spearman ρ = 0.36 (p = 0.005) between recharge potential and observed recharge
DataCopernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover, SoilGrids, IMD rainfall 2014–24, GSI geology and lineaments
