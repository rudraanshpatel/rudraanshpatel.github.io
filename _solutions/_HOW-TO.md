# Adding a solution

1. New note. Name it anything ("Two Sum", "cf 1985B", whatever).
2. Ctrl+P → "Templates: Insert template" → solution. Today's date fills in automatically.
3. Fill in the fields. Only `platform` and `title` are required.
   - platform: LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef, GeeksforGeeks, TUF, AtCoder, CSES...
   - difficulty: Easy / Medium / Hard, or a rating like 1400
   - tags: [sliding-window, two-pointers]
4. Commit and push. The site rebuilds in about a minute.

Rules:
- Keep notes in this folder, not in subfolders (subfolders are ignored).
- Files starting with _ are ignored (like this one).
- No [[wikilinks]] and no pasted images; they won't show on the site.
- Never publish a solution while its contest is still running.
- To log something from a past day, just change the date field.
